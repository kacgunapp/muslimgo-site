MuslimGo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026 · The app's interface is in Turkish; Turkish screen labels are given in parentheses.

1. Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or a password.
  • We do not collect location (GPS, Wi-Fi, location from IP), contacts, photos, microphone data or the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • There are no advertising SDKs and no trackers. We do not sell your data. We do not track you across apps or websites, so we never ask for tracking permission (ATT).
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The server connection for Leagues (Lig) and Friends (Arkadaşlar) (Supabase, European Union) is optional and, on this policy's effective date, is off in the app. While it is off, no data goes to a server. In a version where it is on, opening these screens sends your nickname and a progress backup under an anonymous account; you can delete this data at any time (section 4).
  • Anonymous usage statistics (PostHog, European Union) only count a few actions in Leagues and Friends, so no events are sent while the server connection is off. If they are enabled in the app version, they are on by default; you can turn them off in Friends (Arkadaşlar) → Privacy (Gizlilik) (section 5).
  • When in-app purchases are available, Apple processes the payment and RevenueCat is used to verify purchases (section 6).

2. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only on your phone. Apart from what sections 4–6 describe, none of it is sent to us or anywhere else:

  • Your learning progress: XP, the days you studied, completed lessons, your review list and a daily activity summary.
  • Your answers during setup: goal, level, daily time.
  • Reminder time, quiet hours and the city you choose for prayer times. Prayer times are calculated on your device; no location permission is requested.
  • Sound, haptics and reduced-motion preferences; celebrations you have already seen.
  • Hearts, in-app coins, streak freezes and double-XP status; letter workshop progress.
  • Family plan: the names you type (up to 6 people), the shared goal and the "who studied today" marks. These never leave your device; marks older than 56 days are deleted.
  • Technical records: scheduled notifications, a cache of your subscription status, your anonymous-statistics setting and installation ID, and your server session token (only if you opened Leagues or Friends).

Deleting the app deletes this data. If iPhone backup is on, this data may be included in Apple's device backup (iCloud or computer). That backup is under Apple's and your control; we cannot see it.

3. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, iOS asks for notification permission. Notifications are scheduled and shown on your device. We do not send push notifications from a server and we do not collect a push token. Quiet hours and prayer times are also calculated on your device.

4. Leagues and Friends: optional anonymous account (Supabase)

Connection status: On this policy's effective date, the server connection is off in the app. In that case the Leagues and Friends screen shows that the server is off, no anonymous account is created and none of the data in this section is sent. The rest of this section applies only to a version in which the connection is on.

The first time you open the Leagues or Friends screen, an anonymous account with a random ID is created on Supabase. It has no email address, phone number or password. If you never open these screens, no data about you is created on the server.

With this account, the following data is sent to the server:

  • Your nickname (2–20 characters, only if you type one) and a randomly generated friend code.
  • Your progress backup: XP, the days you studied and completed lesson IDs. It is used to calculate weekly league XP and friend streaks and to merge with the progress on your device.
  • Your league membership: which group you are in for which week and your XP at the start of that week.
  • Your friendships: two anonymous IDs and the request status (pending or accepted).
  • To calculate friend streaks, today's date is sent; this date is not stored.

Who can see what: Only your friends, the members of your league group and the parties of a friend request can see your nickname. League group members see your XP for that week. Your friends see the shared streak, calculated from consecutive days on which you both studied. Someone searching with your friend code cannot see your profile; they only learn whether the request was sent. Everyone can see only their own backup.

Where: Supabase, European Union region. When you connect, your IP address technically reaches Supabase and may be kept in the service's security logs.

How long: Until you delete it.

Deletion: Friends (Arkadaşlar) → Privacy (Gizlilik) → "Delete my data on the server" ("Sunucudaki verimi sil") deletes your anonymous account and all server data linked to it (nickname, friend code, backup, league membership, friendships). The progress on your device remains. If you deleted the app without doing this, email us your friend code or nickname and we will delete the record.

5. Anonymous usage statistics (PostHog)

To understand how the league and friend features are used, anonymous counts of a few actions are sent. This works only if the PostHog connection is enabled in the app version; if it is not, no events are sent and the switch below is not shown. If enabled, it is on by default. You can turn it off with the "Anonymous usage statistics" ("Kimliksiz kullanım istatistiği") switch in Friends (Arkadaşlar) → Privacy (Gizlilik). When you turn it off, the installation ID on your device is deleted and no new events are sent. If you turn it back on, a new ID is created.

All events happen only after a server action in Leagues or Friends: when the league table loads, when your nickname is saved, or when a friend request is sent or accepted. While the server connection is off (section 4), no events are created and no request goes to PostHog.

What is sent:

  • The event name and time. The only events sent are: league table opened, nickname saved, friend request sent, friend request accepted.
  • A random installation ID. It is not linked to an account, to your device identifier or to your Supabase ID.
  • Only these details: the number of people in the league group and the result of a friend request. Your lesson progress, XP and answers are not sent.

Religious texts, your answers and anything you type are never sent. No IP address is added to events, location lookup is disabled and no person profile is created. When you connect, your IP address technically reaches PostHog; IP anonymisation is turned on in MuslimGo's PostHog project, so it is not stored with events. There is no automatic screen capture, session recording or tap tracking.

Where: PostHog's European Union cloud (Frankfurt). How long: 1 year (the retention period of the free PostHog plan we use). PostHog deletes expired events in its regular clean-ups, not immediately. Because events cannot be linked to a person, they cannot be removed one by one.

6. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

This applies when in-app purchases (subscriptions and coin packs) are available. On this policy's effective date, purchases are off in the app. In a version where purchases are off, RevenueCat is never started and makes no network requests.

  • Apple processes the payment. We never see your card or payment details.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) is used to verify purchases and recognise your subscription. RevenueCat receives: an anonymous user ID generated by RevenueCat, your purchase history and App Store receipt, a device identifier (IDFV), your App Store country code, your preferred languages, and the app and SDK versions. Your IP address also reaches RevenueCat when you connect.
  • IDFV is Apple's developer-scoped device identifier. It is not the advertising identifier and is not used to track you. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not collected.
  • The RevenueCat ID is not linked to your Supabase account or to your statistics ID.
  • Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own privacy policies. You manage and cancel your subscription in your App Store settings.

7. Sharing

"Export your data" ("Verini dışa aktar") in Settings (Ayarlar) opens your progress and settings as text in the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes. Apart from that, we do not share, sell or rent your data. Only the service providers named in this policy (Supabase, PostHog, RevenueCat, Apple) process data to run the service.

8. What we do not collect

Your real name (apart from an optional nickname), email address, phone number, postal address, location (GPS, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, location from IP), contacts, photos and camera, microphone and voice recordings, health data, the advertising identifier (IDFA), browsing history and crash reports. The app has no advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDK.

9. International transfers

Supabase and PostHog data is processed in the European Union. When purchases are available, RevenueCat data is processed in the United States. Apple processes payments on its own infrastructure.

10. Legal basis and your rights (GDPR and KVKK)

Backup, leagues and purchase verification are needed to provide the features you choose. Anonymous statistics rely on our legitimate interest in improving the app, and you can turn them off at any time. You have the right to access your data, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. Data on your device is already in your hands. You can delete server data in the app or email us; we reply to emailed requests within 30 days. Anonymous events cannot be linked to a person, so they cannot be extracted per person. You can also contact the data protection authority in your country.

Because we do not ask for a name, email address or phone number, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The nickname in Leagues and Friends is optional. We recommend that children under 13 use these features under parental supervision. If you believe your child has data on our server, email us and we will delete it.
